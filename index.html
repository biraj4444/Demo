Expressive Send

An interaction prototype demonstrating a possible new messaging experience.

A message is typed, sent — and instead of the bubble simply appearing, a small paper plane carries it: launching from the composer, curving toward the person you're messaging, and arriving with a brief expressive moment before the message settles into the thread.

Demo recording of the prototype in motion

How it works

The interaction is one continuous sequence, short enough that it never delays the conversation.

1

Type a message, same as usual.

2

Press send — the bubble doesn't appear right away.

3

A small paper plane lifts off from the composer.

4

It flies toward the recipient on a smooth, slightly curved path.

5

It reaches their avatar and produces a brief arrival effect.

6

The message appears in the thread.

Why this exists

The idea came out of an unrelated experiment: a delivery-style paper-plane animation built for a personal portfolio. Watching it land kept suggesting the same question — what if sending a message could feel like sending something to another person, rather than a bubble simply materializing on screen? This prototype is a small, working answer to that question.
