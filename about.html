Who I am

I'm 20, a college student in Dibrugarh, Assam, India. I'm self-taught, learning full-stack and frontend development by building my own projects rather than through a formal program.

I work primarily from an Android phone, using Termux. Most of what I know has come from building things with whatever I have access to, and I've used AI tools, including Claude, as development assistants along the way. The concept, direction, and decisions in Expressive Send are my own.

Why I made this

Expressive Send grew out of a paper-plane delivery animation I built for a personal project. It made me wonder whether sending a message could feel a little more like sending something to another person. I built a working prototype so the idea could be tried rather than only described.

It is an independent concept, not a production-ready feature, and not affiliated with Meta or Instagram. I'd like to hear what a professional product and design team thinks of it, and the rating box at the bottom of the home page is the quickest way to tell me.
